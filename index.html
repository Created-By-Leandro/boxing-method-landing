Weigh-In Q&A

Before You Sign

Is £40/mo too good to be true?

No catch: I've only been coaching two years, and I'm already getting real results, this is early bird pricing, you're catching me while I'm cheap. Prices go up as my results and track record build. After 20 spots, it moves to £75/mo.

Is £75/mo too much to spend?

Work out what you're already spending on coffee or food each week. Once you're eating properly on this programme, you'll likely spend less on takeaways than you do now, not more.

Is this a one-off payment or does it renew?

I run it monthly, not as a one-off fee for six weeks. Your first month covers the initial 6-week block, then it renews automatically month to month so you can carry on into the next block with me. There's no minimum term, it just keeps running until you tell me you want to stop.

I've tried and failed before, why would this be different?

That's usually not a willpower problem, it's a lack of tools and support from someone who's actually done it and knows the obstacles firsthand, that's me. I've built The Boxing Method in steps over six weeks, one step forward at a time, not a leap you're expected to make on day one.

Do I need a boxing gym or experience?

No. I've built it to run in any commercial gym, and I plan every session for you, no boxing background required.

Which track should I pick?

Weight Cut if the priority is dropping weight; Strength Build if it's getting stronger and more conditioned. Same time commitment either way.

What happens after 6 weeks?

I issue you a new 6-week block and you decide what's next, keep cutting the weight or switch into strength building. Either way, you carry straight on with me, no gap and no starting from scratch.
